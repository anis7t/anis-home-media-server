## 3. The six findings that changed the plan

These came from reading the source, not the handoff summary. Each is now recorded in the spec.

**F1 — The layer stack in the plan document was wrong, and omitted grain.**
Canonical z-order: `veilL/veilR (z2) → seam (z3) → **fx canvas (z4)** → vignette (z5) → grain (z6) →
lockup (z7) → hud (z8, hidden)`. The plan said *"FX canvas → left veil → right veil → seam glow →
vignette → lockup"* — putting the FX canvas **below** the veils and leaving grain out entirely.

**F2 — The Flutter client's entire FX layer is invisible.**
`brand_intro_overlay.dart:141-284` places `_IntroFXPainter` **first** in its `Stack` (the bottom),
under two **opaque** obsidian veils. The light beams, converging particles, impact flash, shockwave
rings, standing brand glow and settling embers — the whole `0.12 – 1.95 s` build-up — are **painted
and then completely covered**. Flutter also has **no grain layer**. This is the largest divergence
and it was **not** in the original drift list, so **Slice 11.4's scope is materially larger** than
"side-by-side lockup + delete a button".

**F3 — `renderAt(t)` is not pure in the original.**
The camera shake uses `Math.random()` (`source: 541-544`) and the veil's 14 s `ambientDrift` is an
infinite CSS animation outside `t` (`source: 39-42, 118`). `capture_overlay_frames.py:77` drives
`INTRO.renderAt(t)`, so the archived PNGs contain a *random* shake and two captures of the same `t`
differ inside the `0.62 – 1.04 s` window. This is why D9 and D10 exist — without them, Slice 11.2's
"frame for frame" oracle is not achievable.

**F4 — The canonical lockup rule is a breakpoint, not "side by side".**
The source toggles `.portrait` on `H > W` (`source: 355`) and stacks the lockup in portrait
(`source: 98-100`). Flutter stacks **unconditionally** (`brand_intro_overlay.dart:347`). So the
**phone in portrait already matches** and it is the **TV (landscape)** that must become side by
side. D6's original phrasing would have broken the phone.

**F5 — Two stale cue times were in the docs.**
`docs/OPENING_SEQUENCE.md` §3 said `curtainEnd` at **2.26**; the source says **2.75**. The phase
plan said impact at `t≈0.55`; the source says **0.62**. A third: the handover overlap is **0.43 s**
(`3.00 − 2.57`), not the "0.38 s" the plan and handoff both claimed.

**F6 — The web's default mode is `auto`, not `gate`.**
`source: 914` is `var qs='auto'`. The old doc's "default `gate`" was the *standalone* page's
default, not the injected overlay's. D5 and the AGENTS.md invariant want `auto`.

**Bonus — `AGENTS.md`'s Flutter invariant was wrong in three ways:** it called the Flutter intro
*"hardware-accelerated portrait video"* (it is a `CustomPaint`; the MP4 is never played), called the
gating *"session gating via IntroController"* (`hasSeenIntro` is set but **never persisted or
consulted**), and advertised the now-removed Replay action.
