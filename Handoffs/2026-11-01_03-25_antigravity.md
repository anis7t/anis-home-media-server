## Root Causes & Implementations

### 1. Catalog Progress SQLx Float Type Coercion Bug
- **Bug**: In PostgreSQL (`media_server`), `progress.position` and `progress.duration` are `FLOAT4` (`real`). In `rust/src/services/catalog/movie_service.rs`, `r.try_get::<f64, _>("position").unwrap_or(0.0)` failed with a SQLx `DecodeError(MismatchedType)` on every query. The `.unwrap_or(0.0)` silently returned `0.0` for all movies in `/api/movies` and `/api/movie/{filename}`.
- **Client Consequence**: In `movie_details_screen.dart`, `hasProgress = movie.position > 10` evaluated to `false`, rendering only "Play Movie" starting at `Duration.zero`.
- **Fix**:
  - In `rust/src/services/catalog/movie_service.rs`, explicitly cast `COALESCE(p.position, 0.0)::DOUBLE PRECISION as position` and `COALESCE(p.duration, 0.0)::DOUBLE PRECISION as duration` across all `list_movies` and `get_movie_details` queries.
  - Added resilient fallback decoding in Rust:
    ```rust
    let position: f64 = r
        .try_get::<f64, _>("position")
        .or_else(|_| r.try_get::<f32, _>("position").map(|v| v as f64))
        .unwrap_or(0.0);
    let duration: f64 = r
        .try_get::<f64, _>("duration")
        .or_else(|_| r.try_get::<f32, _>("duration").map(|v| v as f64))
        .unwrap_or(0.0);
    ```

### 2. Transcode AMF H.264 SPS Level Hardening
- **Bug**: In `rust/src/services/media_pipeline/gpu_service.rs`, `h264_amf` was invoked without explicit profile and level flags. AMD drivers defaulted the H.264 SPS level to `-99`, which Android `MediaCodec` on Fire TV rejected as an invalid stream.
- **Fix**: Added explicit universal profile and level flags to `h264_amf` (`-profile:v high -level 4.1`) and `hevc_amf` (`-profile:v main`).

### 3. Flutter MediaKit Player Adapter Stabilization
- **Bug 1**: `videoController.waitUntilFirstFrameRendered` only completes once. On quality switches, `.then()` fired immediately on the completed future, cancelling the 4-second timeout and preventing `fallbackToSoftwareDecoder()`.
- **Bug 2**: Opening an HLS URL with `Media(url, start: currentPos)` made libmpv sequentially fetch chunks from chunk 0 over HTTP, stalling the player.
- **Fix**:
  - In `flutter_client/lib/features/player/data/media_kit_player_adapter.dart`, open HLS streams without `start` in `Media(...)`, and dispatch `player.seek(startPosition)` after opening.
  - Added a stream-level watchdog subscription `_frameWatchdogSub` to confirm active rendering across quality changes.
  - Expanded `player.stream.error` listener to catch `hardware`, `omx`, and `c2.` errors.
