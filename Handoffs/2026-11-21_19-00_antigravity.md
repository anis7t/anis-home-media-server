1. **Live Dual-GPU Telemetry on Transcode Dashboards (Web & Mobile)**:

   - Built React 19 production bundle (`web/dist/`) and compiled optimized release binary `rust/target/release/media-server-api.exe`.

2. **Investigation of Active Transcode Pause/Resume Cycle (Job 1150)**:
   - **Root Cause Identified**: At 18:46:58 IST, while encoding chunk 50, the discrete Radeon RX 560X GPU temperature hit the critical safety threshold of **92.0°C**.
   - **Thermal Protection Guard Fired**: The active thermal monitor safely terminated FFmpeg to prevent hardware overheating or thermal trip.
   - **Rapid Cooldown & Resumption**: Within 10 seconds of process termination, GPU temperature dropped from 92°C to 69°C.
   - **Checkpoint Resumption**: `transcode_sweeper` picked up the job, incremented `attempts` from 1 to 2, and seamlessly resumed chunk 50 onward without discarding any previously encoded segments (chunks 0–49).
   - **Current Status**: Job 1150 is actively running smoothly at **~33% complete** (~57 minutes encoded) at ~0.85x speed.
