## 2. The decisions

| # | Decision | Answer |
|---|---|---|
| **D17** | The player | **`audioplayers` 6.8.1** — a pure-Dart wrapper over Android's `MediaPlayer`. Small, and its platform interface makes the host tests a fake, so the suite never touches a real player. `just_audio` is built for streaming; `soundpool` is for rapid-fire game SFX; `media_kit`, though already a dependency, is a **video** engine whose **host** backend is the libmpv that Issue 11 is about. |
| **D18** | The `m` key | **Any key dismisses, as the Flutter client does today.** So the Flutter client has **no** `m` mute, and the score is silenced by the device's own volume. A divergence from the web, recorded as **L18**. |
| **D19** | The score on every launch | **Yes — the canonical behaviour.** The intro plays on every launch (D15) and the score is part of it, so the app makes a sound on every cold launch. |

## 3. The contract

The web's (§7.9), with the platform's differences taken into account:

| Situation | What happens |
|---|---|
| The player is ready when the sequence starts | The score starts at the sequence's t = 0 |
| The player is **not** ready — Android's preparation took 80 ms | The score starts when it is ready, **anchored to the sequence's position at that moment** — the same anchoring as the web |
| The sequence is over by then | **The score is dropped** — a sound out of nowhere is worse than no sound |
| The sequence is dismissed | The score stops, and it cannot outlive the overlay |
| Always | **The animation never waits for the audio** — the score is prepared before the first frame, and nothing awaits it |

**Nothing in the player is allowed to throw.** A device with no audio, a platform channel that is
not registered, a player that fails to prepare — all of them mean the same thing: the sequence
plays, silently.

## 4. The asset

The canonical's file, **byte for byte**. The test suite asserts the SHA-256 of the shipped WAV
against `docs/opening-sequence/assets/intro-theme.wav`, and reads the WAV's header to assert
44.1 kHz, 16-bit, stereo, 3.070 s.

**The dead video is gone.** `assets/videos/intro-demo-portrait.mp4` was declared in the pubspec,
**never played**, and cost **2.43 MB** in every APK. It is gone from the pubspec and from the
tree; the asset section now names exactly one file, and a test says so. The APK's assets shrink by
about **1.9 MB**.

## 5. The divergences, recorded

**L18** — the Flutter client has **no unlocking gesture** (Android has no autoplay policy, so the
score starts on its own — D19) and **no `m` mute** (any key dismisses, as it always has — D18).

Together with **L3** (the grain) and **L17** (the handover), those are the three rows in the
ledger that are differences from the web, and all three are stated rather than hidden.
