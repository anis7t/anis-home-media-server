   - Rust Axum connection pool timed out (`PoolTimedOut`) after 30s while waiting for PostgreSQL to recover.
   - Verified that `MediaServerPG` has SCM failure restart policies configured (`restart/60000`), allowing automatic recovery once the drive reconnected.
