## 1. Executive Summary & Deliverables

In this session, we addressed the critical distinction between fatal media failures and thermal safeguard pauses:

1. **Problem Resolved**:
   - The transcode sweeper's circuit breaker (`attempts >= 3`) was originally designed to prevent infinite loops on corrupt bitstreams or broken video files.
   - However, when the thermal protection guard halted FFmpeg at $\ge 92^\circ\text{C}$, the job was marked `failed`, incrementing `attempts`. On long 4K movies requiring multiple cooldowns, this would prematurely trip the circuit breaker and abandon the transcode.
2. **Architecture Decoupling Implemented**:
   - **`gpu_thermal.rs`**: Defined `THERMAL_PAUSE_MARKER = "[THERMAL_PAUSE]"` and `is_thermal_safeguard_error(err)`.
   - **`job_service.rs`**: In `JobService::fail()`, if the error is a thermal safeguard pause, `attempts` is decremented back (`attempts = GREATEST(0, attempts - 1)`), resulting in **zero attempt penalty**.
   - **`transcode_sweeper.rs`**: Thermal safeguard pauses are explicitly **exempt** from the 3-attempt circuit breaker, granting them **unlimited retries** following system cooldown.
   - **`transcode_worker.rs`**: Thermal stops and telemetry requirements explicitly tag errors with `[THERMAL_PAUSE]`.
   - **React 19 SPA (`TranscodeDashboardPage.tsx`)**: Renders a dedicated amber `Thermal Cooldown` pill badge instead of a generic red `Failed` badge.
   - **Flutter Client (`transcode_dashboard_view.dart`)**: Added `error` to `TranscodeRecentJob` and renders `THERMAL COOLDOWN` with `Icons.thermostat_rounded` in warning amber.
3. **Active Transcode Status (Job 1150)**:
   - Reset `attempts = 0` on Job 1150 (*The Odyssey*, 4K UHD BluRay).
   - Confirmed live recovery: At chunk 80 (~46.5%), a thermal stop cooled the GPU down from 92°C to 65°C, sweeper auto-resumed, and Job 1150 is currently actively running at **~48% complete** with `attempts = 1`.
