How to Use Anis' Home Media Server

Everything you need to know about streaming your movies, navigating the modern player, using hardware multi-GPU acceleration, managing subtitles, and uploading files.

1. Getting Started & Remote Access

Anis' Home Media Server is a private, lightweight streaming suite accessible from any web browser on your phone, tablet, laptop, or desktop computer. You can also install it as a Progressive Web App (PWA) for an immersive, full-screen app icon experience on iOS and Android.

🏠 Local Network

Access high-speed, zero-compression streaming at http://127.0.0.1:8000 or your server's LAN IP when connected to your home Wi-Fi.

🌐 Remote Anywhere

Stream securely outside your house through the named Cloudflare Tunnel at https://media.anisparvez.in without port-forwarding or CGNAT headaches.

📲 PWA Installation

In Safari (iOS) tap Share → Add to Home Screen; in Chrome (Android/Desktop) click the install prompt in the address bar.

🎬

2. Browsing & Library Exploration

The library view provides instant search, continue watching progress, and movie cards with rich metadata enriched automatically by TMDb:

  • Instant Search: Type any title or keyword into the top search bar. Matches filter dynamically across titles, release years, and movie genres.
  • Continue Watching: Movies you recently started but haven't finished appear in the top rail cards with red progress indicators. Clicking any card immediately resumes from your exact saved position.
  • Movie Details Page: Clicking any poster brings up the movie hero with full synopsis, release details, star ratings, genres, technical stream codecs (resolution, audio channels, bitrates), and an interactive cast avatar strip.
▶️

3. Video Player Masterclass

The HTML5 player combines desktop precision with ergonomic touch interactions designed specifically for handheld phones and tablets:

🖼️ Live Hover Thumbnails

Hovering over or scrubbing the YouTube-style layered seekbar extracts on-demand frame previews so you can preview scenes before seeking.

👆 Tap-to-Reveal Controls

A single tap on the video reveals playback controls without toggling play/pause. Controls auto-hide after 2.5s of inactivity.

📱 Double-Tap Seeking

Double-tapping the left or right third of the player rewinds or fast-forwards by 10s with animated radial ripple bubbles.

🔄 Aspect Ratio & Rotation

Cycle through Best Fit, 16:9, 4:3, and Crop using the aspect button, or toggle 90° hardware video rotation.

💡 Pro Tip: Click the elapsed time (0:00) on the bottom left to toggle between elapsed playback time and negative remaining time (e.g. -42:15).

5. Direct Play & Dual-GPU Transcoding

The media server dynamically picks the fastest, most resource-efficient delivery mode for every file:

⚡ Direct Play (RFC 7233)

Standard MP4/WebM files containing H.264 video and AAC audio stream with zero server CPU/GPU conversion using zero-copy OS range requests for instant seeking.

🎮 Dynamic Dual-GPU Transcoding

MKV and HEVC (H.265) files are segmented into keyframe-aligned chunks and split across both the discrete AMD Radeon RX 560X and integrated AMD Vega 8 simultaneously.

During active transcoding, an in-stream progress pill and progress card report real-time encoding percentage, encoding speed (e.g. 2.8×), and estimated completion time (ETA).

💬

6. Subtitles & Language Auto-Detection

Never struggle with out-of-sync or awkwardly positioned captions again:

  • Dual-Axis Positioning: Customize horizontal alignment (Center, Left, Right) and vertical elevation (Lowered Bottom, Bottom, Raised, Middle, Top). Subtitles dynamically elevate to avoid colliding with playback controls.
  • In-Player & Server Subtitle Upload: You can upload .srt or .vtt files directly from the details page or from within the video player's Subtitle Settings modal (⚙).
  • Intelligent Language Auto-Detection: Uploaded subtitles are parsed for character scripts (Latin, Devanagari, Hanzi, Cyrillic, Arabic, Bengali) and NLP stop-word frequency to classify the language automatically with zero manual renaming required.
📤

7. Uploading & Ingestion

Adding movies to your server is effortless:

  1. Click the prominent Upload button in the top navigation header on any page.
  2. Drag and drop any video file (.mp4, .mkv, .webm, .mov, .avi, .m4v) into the upload drop zone.
  3. Optional: If the file came from a phone with a numeric name (e.g. 1000403712.mkv), provide a Movie Title in the rename box.
  4. Click Upload Media. Large files transfer via resumable chunks with real-time transfer rate smoothing and accurate ETA calculation.
  5. Once finished, the animated ingestion card automatically probes video streams, queries TMDb for posters/backdrops/metadata, and adds the title to your library.
💾

8. Storage Tiering & Cache Retention

To preserve maximum performance while accommodating massive movie collections, the server uses a tiered multi-volume design:

  • Primary Fast Drive (C: NVMe SSD): Reserved for operating system, database (media.db), live seek previews, and ready-to-stream HLS cache.
  • Secondary Storage (D:\Flicks): Dedicated mass storage holding your full raw video files, upload staging, and cold archives.
  • One-Click Cache Cleaning: Visit My Library (/manage) to audit storage pools, inspect transcode cache footprints, and safely purge orphaned HLS caches with a single click.
📱

9. Connected Devices & Network Visibility

The Connected Devices dashboard (/devices) gives you complete transparency into who and what is connected:

  • Device Telemetry: Identifies exact hardware models (e.g. Apple iPhone, Samsung Galaxy, Google Pixel) using Chromium High-Entropy Client Hints.
  • Network Classification: Distinguishes between local home LAN connections and remote WAN tunnel sessions with public ISP identification.
  • Friendly Renaming: Click Rename on any device to give it a custom name (e.g. Anis' iPad Pro or Living Room Apple TV).
  • Per-Device Watch History: View past watched titles and resume timestamps for each device.

10. Frequently Asked Questions

Why does an MKV file take a few seconds to start playing?
Web browsers cannot natively decode MKV containers. When you press play, the server's dual-GPU transcode engine immediately begins converting the video into browser-friendly HLS fMP4 segments. Once the first few seconds are processed, playback begins immediately while the rest encodes in the background.
How do I fix subtitles that appear too low or collide with controls?
Open the Subtitle Settings modal by clicking the gear icon (⚙) next to CC in the player controls or press c. Set Vertical Position to Raised Bottom or Middle.
Can I watch movies when away from home?
Yes! Navigate to https://media.anisparvez.in on your mobile device or laptop. The server communicates through Cloudflare's secure named tunnel without needing a public static IP or open firewall ports.
